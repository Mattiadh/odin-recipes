Vegan Thai Curry

A delicious vegan curry using red curry paste, vegetables and tofu.

This is the best food ever. 10/10. The trick is to start cooking before you're hungry, as it can take a while to pre-fry the tofu and put together the curry flavours.

Ingredients
  • Red curry paste
  • tofu
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Coconut milk
  • Vegan fish sauce
  • Jasmin rice
Steps
  1. Cut the tofu into cubes. Fry with oil in a pan until crispy.
  2. Chop vegetables and put to the side.
